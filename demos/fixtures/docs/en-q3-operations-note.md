The fleet grew from 41 to **47 trucks** during the quarter. Six refrigerated
trucks were added for the Eastern Province route.

The new **Dammam depot opened on 12 August 2026**. It has 18 loading bays and
serves the Eastern Province and Bahrain causeway routes.

- On-time delivery rate: **93.4 %** (target 92 %).
- Damaged-goods claims: 0.7 % of shipments.
- Average customer response time: 3 hours 20 minutes.
