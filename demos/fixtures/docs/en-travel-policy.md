# Falcon Ridge Logistics — Travel and Expense Policy

**SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT.** Falcon Ridge Logistics does not exist. Every
name, number and date below is invented for the AI Stack 2026 demos.

Policy code: FRL-TE-2026-07. Effective from 1 July 2026. Owner: the Finance
Director.

## 1. Who this applies to

Every employee and contractor of Falcon Ridge Logistics who travels for work
inside or outside the Kingdom.

## 2. Daily allowances

- Meals: **185 SAR per day** inside the Kingdom, 240 SAR per day abroad.
- Hotel cap in Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam: **650 SAR per night**. Other cities: 480 SAR per night.
- Airport taxi or ride-hailing: actual cost, with a receipt.

## 3. Approval

Any single trip that will cost more than **4,000 SAR** in total needs written
approval from the Finance Director **before** booking. Trips under that amount
need only the line manager's approval.

## 4. Claims

Submit expense claims within **14 calendar days** of returning. Claims
submitted later are paid only with the Finance Director's written exception.
Lost receipts above 200 SAR need a signed declaration.

## 5. What is never reimbursed

Fines, upgrades to business class that were not approved in writing, minibar
charges, and personal entertainment.
