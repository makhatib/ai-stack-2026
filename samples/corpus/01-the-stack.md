# What a self-hosted AI stack is for

A self-hosted stack trades convenience for three things: your data stays on
hardware you control, your costs are a one-off rather than per-token, and
nothing you type is training anybody else's model.

The trade is real in both directions. A local model on a CPU answers slowly,
and the quality of a four-billion-parameter model is not the quality of a
frontier one. Anyone who tells you otherwise is selling something.
